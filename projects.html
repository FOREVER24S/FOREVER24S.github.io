Industry 3

High-voltage power distribution, battery systems and flash charging.

Flash charging: the battery and the distribution box in the charging pile

High-power charging changes what the hardware has to survive: continuous high current, heat, contactor and fuse sizing, connector duty, and a fault path that has to stay safe at power levels a household supply never sees. On a supported vehicle the system charges the battery from 10% to 70% state of charge in five minutes, and from 10% to 97% in nine - 12 minutes at -30 °C, only three minutes more than the standard time. If charging stops being the reason people hesitate to buy an EV, this is the technology that did it.

Ph.D. research 3

Biodegradable metals, devices and additive manufacturing: the record underneath.

Neuro-interventional devices

Four devices for the vessels in the brain, built on biodegradable molybdenum: an ultra-fine wire braided implant for stroke treatment, a self-expanding radiopaque stent for intracranial atherosclerotic stenosis, a 3D detachable coil for aneurysm embolization, and a Nitinol stent retriever that reached 80 clinical cases. Alloy design and wire processing ran alongside the device work, because a braided implant is only ever as good as the metal it is braided from.

Orthopaedic implants and additive manufacturing

Biodegradable metal scaffolds for large bony defects, printed by laser powder bed fusion, plus the magnesium and zinc alloy work underneath them: severe plastic deformation to refine the grain structure, in vitro corrosion and cell response, and in vivo osteogenesis in animal models. The scaffold has to be printable, hold its shape under load, and degrade at a rate the bone can follow.
